Daily Driver Recipe

On a 24 GB Mac, start here:

w4+kv_cache+prefill

on llama3-8b, mistral-7b, or qwen-7b.

Expect roughly 5 GB peak and 18–21 tok/s on M3.

python scripts/run_benchmark.py --preset llama3-8b --config w4+kv_cache+prefill --hardware "Mac M3"
